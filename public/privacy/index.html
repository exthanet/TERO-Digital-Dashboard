Privacy Policy

TERO Digital Dashboard · last updated 29 October 2026

What this app is

TERO Digital Dashboard is an internal reporting tool of TERO Digital. It is used only by TERO Digital staff to follow the performance of the company's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

Google and YouTube data we access

With the permission of a TERO Digital account that manages the company's YouTube channels (YouTube Content Manager), the app reads, read-only, through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API:

  • channel and video information (titles, publish dates) of the company's own channels;
  • channel and video statistics such as views, watch time, likes and comments;
  • estimated revenue of the company's own channels, for internal and accounting reports.

The app never uploads, edits or deletes anything on YouTube, and it does not access personal data of viewers.

How the data is used

The data is used only to build the dashboard and monthly reports for TERO Digital staff. It is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.

Storage and security

Reports are stored in the company's Google Cloud (Firebase) project and can be opened only by signed-in TERO Digital staff with the right permission. The access token is kept on the server side and is never shown in the app.

Your choices

The account that gave permission can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored reports can be deleted on request. TERO Digital Dashboard's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
